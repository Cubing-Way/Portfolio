Who I Am

I mainly work with JavaScript and frontend application development. I enjoy taking an idea or a specific problem and turning it into something functional that people can actually use.

I originally started coding out of curiosity. I wanted to know whether programming was really as difficult as it seemed, so I asked ChatGPT to teach me something. It introduced me to the combination of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and I became interested in transforming ideas into working applications.

Since then, I've focused less on memorizing syntax and more on understanding how code works, how different parts of an application interact, and how to guide a project toward a desired result.

How I Build

I make use of whatever resources are available to turn an idea into a working project. My approach is not based on knowing every piece of syntax beforehand, but on understanding what needs to happen and figuring out how to make it happen.

This allows me to take a project from an initial idea, through functionality and interface design, to a working application even when the available resources are limited.

For simple problems, I can usually look up the specific functionality I need. For more complex implementations, I prefer to understand the steps and logic first, then use AI assistance where it is useful for implementation.

What I Bring to a Project

My strongest ability is taking an idea and figuring out how to turn it into an application. I can work from the initial concept, design the functionality and interface, build the project with the tools available to me, and continue refining it until it becomes a usable result.

I am particularly interested in projects where the purpose and needs of the end user matter. Understanding why something should exist helps me decide what the application should actually do.

Learning & Background

I have explored programming through practical projects, beginning with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript and gradually moving toward more complex applications and reusable functionality.
